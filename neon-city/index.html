Neon-City

A web-forward city experience — lights, rhythm, and systems layered into one cohesive world Apexnova is building for launch.

What it is

Neon-City is Apexnova’s flagship creative-technical project: a stylized urban setting meant to be explored and felt, not only scrolled past. The focus is on atmosphere, clarity of interaction, and a foundation that can grow into a full public release.

This is the preproduction story — the phase where identity, pacing, and core flows are proven before the production site replaces this preview.

World

Visual language, layout, and motion that sell the idea of a living neon metropolis.

Systems

Underlying structure so features can ship without rewriting the whole experience.

Launch

A path from private iteration to a production-ready public surface when the work is ready.

Where things stand

A separate Neon-City website already exists for experiments and internal builds. It is not the production destination yet. This GitHub Pages pitch exists so collaborators and early visitors understand the project before the final public launch.

Not production. Interfaces, URLs, and content may change without notice until the official release is announced here and on the repository.
