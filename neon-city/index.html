Neon-City

The flagship monorepo for digital infrastructure: a synthwave “mainframe” where districts hold active work, prototypes, automation, archives, and sensitive material — plus Project Neon surfaces built for the open web.

What it is

In the repository, Neon-City is organized as a city: districts separate the power core (configs and sync), downtown high-traffic projects, suburbs for experiments, transit-style automation, a vault for sensitive material, and archives for retired work. The aesthetic layer is synthwave / Project Neon — cyan, pink, purple, and grid orange on a deep void background.

The Synth Wave HTML in the repo is part of that language (dashboards, landing, logs, settings). This GitHub Pages URL is a thin preproduction layer on the web — the full mainframe lives in the repository, with every district and script.

Districts

Clear zones in one monorepo so experiments, production code, and sensitive paths do not collide.

Neon UI

Synthwave surfaces and tokens designed to carry across pages and future public launches.

Scale

Room to grow from personal operator grid to whatever public “city” experience ships next.

Where things stand

In-repo HTML and any separate deploy are experiments and previews, not a promised final public destination until a launch is called out on the repository and here. The README’s district map and recent transmissions on GitHub track what changed last.

Not production. Interfaces, URLs, and content may change without notice until the official release is announced here and on the repository.
